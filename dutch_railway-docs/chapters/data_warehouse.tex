What are the contents of your fact table?
The dimensional model follows the galaxy schema design pattern, while following principles used in the snow
schema design as well. THere are 2 fact tables, fact_service that denotes one schedule/ride instance, while fact_stop
denotes when a train stops at a station. fact_service contains rdt_id as its primary key with date_key as its foreign key.
The columns first_train_number, last_train_number, and train_ID_changed indicate when a service switches trains halfway
through, company_name denotes the company handling that particular service, and cancel_status is as ENUM that denotes
services as partially cancelled, completely cancelled, or no cancellation.

The fact_stop table contains stop_ID as its PK, with RDT_ID and station_code as foreign keys. Additional columns include
the stop's arrival and departure times, train_ID to indicate the train that arrived, a next_day flag that denotes when
a stop arrives the day after it departed, arrival and departure delay computations, and a cancel_status ENUM.
